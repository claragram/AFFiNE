Frequently asked questions

What is Claranote?

Claranote is the open-source, local-first knowledge workspace that combines documents, whiteboards, and AI in one canvas. Claranote Cloud is hosted by our team. Licensing varies by release and component. (Not to be confused with affine geometry in mathematics.)

Is Claranote free?

Yes. Claranote Cloud Free includes unlimited local workspaces, 10 GB of Cloud Storage, real-time collaboration with up to 3 members per workspace, and 7 days of version history. There is no time limit on the Free plan. For self-hosting, review the license terms for your exact release and components.

What is the difference between Claranote Cloud and Self-Hosted?

Claranote Cloud runs on our infrastructure with no setup. Storage, seats, and version-history limits depend on your plan (Free, Pro, or Team). Self-Hosted runs the full stack on your own servers. Licensing depends on the release and components. You operate the servers and manage data stored there; external AI and other connected services process data separately under their own terms. Capacity depends on your infrastructure and selected services. The Self-Hosted Team tier ($10/seat/month, billed annually) adds priority support, multi-admin roles, and scaling for 10+ seats.

Can I use Claranote as a free Notion alternative?

Yes. Claranote's editor is MIT-licensed; Claranote is local-first by design and free for personal use. As an open-source Notion alternative, your workspace data lives on your device with optional cloud sync. For self-hosting, review the license terms for your exact release and components.

What is included in the Claranote Free plan?

Local side: unlimited local workspaces, unlimited customization, unlimited Doc and Edgeless editing. Cloud Basic side: 10 GB of Cloud Storage, 10 MB maximum file size, up to 3 members per Workspace, 7 days of Cloud Time Machine version history, and up to 3 login devices.

What is the Claranote Believer Plan?

The Believer Plan is a one-time purchase of $499.99 that gives you lifetime personal usage of Claranote Pro, including everything in Pro plus 1 TB of Cloud Storage. It is a single payment with no subscription, designed for early supporters who want to back the project long-term.

Can I self-host Claranote for free?

Self-hosting terms depend on the edition, release and components you deploy. Open-source components do not require an EE subscription. The editor (BlockSuite) is MIT-licensed. Review the component licenses before deployment.

What license is Claranote on GitHub?

Claranote uses component-specific licenses. In v0.27.4, the root LICENSE applies MIT outside its listed exceptions. The backend/common-native terms assign MPL 2.0 to CE and specified client-side portions, and EE terms to the remaining covered code. Third-party licenses still apply. Check the terms for your exact release and intended use. Full source at github.com/toeverything/Claranote.

Can Claranote replace Confluence for self-hosted team knowledge bases?

Claranote is a candidate for teams evaluating a self-hosted team knowledge base or corporate wiki. It combines documents, whiteboards and AI in a local-first workspace with an MIT-licensed editor. The Self-Hosted Team tier ($10/seat/month, billed annually) includes priority support, multi-admin roles and unlimited team members; check the selected plan’s current terms. You operate your own infrastructure, while external AI and connected services process data separately under their own terms when used. Before replacing Confluence, validate content and permission migration, governance requirements and integration workflows.
